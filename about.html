About

Simple guidance for complicated water problems

Grounded Home Solutions was created to help Maryland homeowners better understand basement water, crawlspace moisture, sump pumps, drainage concerns, and water movement around the home. We believe homeowners make better decisions when they understand the problem before choosing the solution.

What we focus on

Water problems often overlap. Basement moisture may connect to grading. Crawlspace humidity may connect to drainage. A sump pump concern may point to a larger water-management issue. Grounded Home Solutions exists to help make those connections easier to understand.

Crawlspaces Humidity, insulation, and moisture concerns.
