Basement Waterproofing

Basement water problems are one of the most common homeowner concerns in Maryland, especially in older homes throughout Baltimore, Harford County, and surrounding areas.

Water intrusion can come from:

  • Foundation cracks
  • Poor exterior drainage
  • Hydrostatic pressure
  • Failed sump pumps
  • Improper grading
  • Clogged gutters and downspouts

Signs You May Need Basement Waterproofing

  • Water on basement floors
  • Damp basement walls
  • Musty smells
  • White chalky stains on concrete walls
  • Mold or mildew growth
  • Water after heavy rain

Common Waterproofing Solutions

The right solution depends on the source of the moisture problem. Some homes may benefit from:

  • Interior drainage systems
  • Sump pump installation
  • Exterior grading improvements
  • French drains
  • Crawlspace moisture control
  • Foundation crack repair
