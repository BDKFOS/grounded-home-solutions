Common basement water warning signs

Basement water issues are often easier to address when the source is identified early.

Water after heavy rain Puddles, seepage, or damp flooring after storms.
Damp or stained walls Moisture marks, peeling paint, or white mineral staining.
Musty basement smell Odors that may point to moisture, humidity, or poor ventilation.
Sump pump concerns Constant running, backup worries, or pump failure concerns.

Possible Causes

Why basements get wet

The right waterproofing solution depends on where the water is coming from and why it is collecting around or under the home.

Poor Exterior Drainage

Water may collect near the foundation when grading, gutters, or downspouts are not moving water away properly.

Foundation Cracks

Cracks in walls or floors can allow water to enter during heavy rain or when soil pressure increases.

Hydrostatic Pressure

Water pressure around the foundation can push moisture through weak points in the basement.

Sump Pump Problems

A weak, missing, clogged, or overwhelmed sump pump can leave a basement vulnerable during storms.
