Common yard drainage warning signs

Water outside the home often becomes a bigger problem when it collects near the foundation or has nowhere to drain.

Standing water Puddles that remain long after rain stops.
Soggy lawn areas Soft, muddy, or unusable sections of the yard.
Water near foundation Pooling around the home, basement walls, or crawlspace area.
Downspout problems Gutters or downspouts dumping water too close to the house.

Possible Solutions

What yard drainage work may include

The right fix depends on grading, soil, runoff patterns, downspouts, and where water is collecting.

French Drains

Subsurface drainage systems may help move water away from low or saturated areas.

Downspout Extensions

Redirecting roof runoff can reduce water collecting near the foundation.

Grading Improvements

Improving slope may help water flow away from the home instead of toward it.

Drainage Planning

Some yards need a larger water-management plan instead of a single quick fix.

What To Do Next

How to approach a drainage issue

1

Watch where water collects

Notice whether water pools near the house, in low spots, by downspouts, or along walkways.

2

Look at runoff sources

Rooflines, gutters, neighboring grades, patios, driveways, and compacted soil can all affect drainage.

3

Request help

Describe the drainage problem and where the water is collecting so the next step is clearer.
