Common sump pump warning signs

Sump pump issues often show up during heavy rain, power outages, or when the basement drainage system is under stress.

Pump runs constantly The system may be overloaded, stuck, undersized, or dealing with too much water.
Basement water returns Water near the pit, floor edges, or walls may point to drainage or pump issues.
No backup protection Power outages during storms can leave a basement vulnerable without a backup system.
Strange noises or cycling Grinding, short cycling, or unusual sounds may mean the pump needs attention.

What sump pump help may include

The right next step depends on whether the problem is the pump itself, the pit, discharge line, backup power, or the drainage system feeding water into the sump.

New Pump Installation

A properly sized sump pump can help move collected water away from the basement.

Battery Backup

Backup protection may help when storms cause power outages or the main pump fails.

Discharge Line Review

The discharge path matters because water needs to move safely away from the foundation.

Drainage System Check

Some sump pump problems are really drainage problems feeding too much water into the pit.
