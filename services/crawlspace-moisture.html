Common crawlspace moisture warning signs

Moisture issues under a home often show up indirectly before homeowners notice the source.

Musty smells Odors moving into living spaces.
Damp insulation Insulation holding moisture.
Humidity Moist indoor air and condensation.
Standing water Water appearing after rain.

What moisture control may include

The right next step depends on humidity, drainage, ventilation, insulation, and water entry.

Encapsulation

Create separation from moisture.

Drainage

Manage collected water.

Dehumidification

Reduce moisture levels.

Insulation

Improve comfort and performance.
