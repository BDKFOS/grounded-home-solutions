FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers to common questions about basement waterproofing, crawlspaces, sump pumps, and drainage concerns.

Basement & Waterproofing

What causes water in a basement?

Basement water can come from grading, runoff, foundation pressure, drainage concerns, gutters, or sump pump issues.

Do all wet basements need waterproofing?

Not necessarily. Some moisture issues begin outside the home and may involve drainage or water movement.

Crawlspaces

What causes crawlspace humidity?

Humidity may come from ground moisture, ventilation patterns, drainage concerns, or standing water.

Is encapsulation always necessary?

Not always. The correct approach depends on moisture source and overall conditions.

Drainage & Sump Pumps

What does a sump pump do?

A sump pump helps move collected water away from the home.

Can standing water affect a foundation?

Water movement around a structure may contribute to moisture and drainage concerns over time.

What is a French drain?

French drains are one method used to help redirect collected water.
